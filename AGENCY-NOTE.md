## What it is

A free, single-page tool for a person locked out of an account. They write what happened, count the **bot death-spiral** (each bot reply with no named person), ask the company for its own security records, and get copy-ready letters for the company and for the regulator in their country.

Nothing they type leaves their browser. There is no account, no payment, and no server that sees the form.

## Why we built it

People are losing time, money, care, and work while company help-desks answer with bots across many cases and no named person. The same shape shows up when a login method appears that the person never set up, when the only login code arrives on a channel that is down, and when someone then offers to “get them back in” for a fee.

This page does not recover accounts. It gives the person a record and the right doors.
